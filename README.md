## 🧠 About Me

I am deeply interested in understanding **how intelligence emerges from biological neural systems** and translating those principles into **efficient artificial intelligence models**.

My work sits at the intersection of:

- **Computational Neuroscience**
- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning**
- **Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)**
- **Neuromorphic & Energy-Efficient AI**
- **Brain–Computer Interfaces (BCI)**

I enjoy **reverse-engineering neural learning mechanisms** — from membrane potentials to synaptic plasticity — and implementing them computationally.
